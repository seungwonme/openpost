title: Reply to Instagram messages
description: Answer an Instagram direct message that contains your keyword, once, while the messaging window is open.

Use this workflow when people message your account with a keyword such as `GUIDE`. It sends your saved text to the person who asked. It does not need an AI provider and it never messages anyone who has not messaged you.

## Before you start

Connect an Instagram professional account and turn on **Messages** for it. The trigger reads messages stored in Inbox, so the account's messaging permissions and Meta webhook subscription must already deliver them.

## Create the workflow

Open **Workflows → Templates → Reply to Instagram keyword messages**. The draft opens in the workflow editor.

1. Open the trigger and choose the Instagram account.
2. Enter keywords separated by commas. Matching ignores capitalization, and the default matches whole words or phrases. Leave the field empty to match every message.
3. Open the **Reply to message** step and write the message, up to 1,000 bytes. Variables such as `{{source.author_name}}` count after they render.
4. Keep **Review in Inbox** while you check the wording, or choose **Send automatically**.
5. Select **Publish workflow** to activate it. Creating, saving and previewing the draft send nothing.

## Narrow the trigger

Open **Configure** in the trigger to exclude keywords, choose whole-word, exact or contains matching, change the daily run limit, or adjust reply priority. Lower priority numbers answer first when several reply workflows match the same message, and only one replies. Equal priorities use the oldest workflow. A workflow that sends nothing can still run independently.

Only messages created and first stored after activation start runs. Earlier messages, imported history, your own messages and messages from another account you connected do not. Reactivating a paused workflow starts with future messages again. New messages start a run within about five minutes.

## Review or take over

Open the conversation in **Inbox → Messages** to see each automatic message and its state. Workspace administrators can edit a message waiting for review and approve it. Editors can select **Take over**.

**Take over** stops pending automatic messages in that conversation. So does sending a manual reply there, in OpenPost or in the Instagram app. Automation stays stopped in that conversation until someone selects **Resume automation**, which applies to future messages. Messages already being sent may still arrive.

**Pause** stops new runs and leaves existing runs active. **Stop automatic replies** stops new runs and pending sends.

## What stops a message

OpenPost checks again just before sending, including after a review or a queue delay. The run fails and shows the reason when any of these is true:

- Instagram's messaging window closed. The window normally lasts 24 hours from the person's latest message. OpenPost does not use message tags to extend it.
- The person opted out.
- A person took over the conversation.
- The person deleted the message.
- The account lost its connection or its messaging permission.

A delay of 24 hours or more before a reply step fails when you publish, because that message could never be sent.

People who send `STOP`, `unsubscribe`, `stop replying`, `그만` or `수신거부` as a whole message are opted out of automatic messages from that account, and the opt-out also covers its comment automation.

Failed and unconfirmed sends remain inspectable. OpenPost does not repeat a send when the outcome is uncertain. One message gets at most one automatic reply, even if Meta delivers its webhook twice. A conversation that receives five automatic replies in an hour stops receiving them until the hour passes.

See [Meta's messaging documentation](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/instagram-platform/instagram-api-with-instagram-login/messaging-api) for provider requirements. This workflow does not include follower gates, email capture or broadcasts.
